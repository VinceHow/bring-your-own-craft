# The cast

Hand-drawn creature teammates in warm gouache (lineup.jpg is the reference every sheet was painted from).
Names are the ones the demo script already uses. Each has one prop for their craft and one signature move.
No pronouns, as the vision asks.

| | Mika · Design | Noor · Research | Theo · Data | Ines · Strategy | Bo · Engineering |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shape | Tall, wobbly teardrop, ink blue | Perfectly round, terracotta, round glasses | Small, neat, upright, sage | Wide, grounded pear, gold ochre | Chunky soft loaf, butter yellow, half-lidded eyes |
| Personality | Quick, playful, restless; always doodling | Curious, careful, warm; a source for every claim | Calm, precise, quiet, tidy; one number that says it worked | Confident, kind, grounded; who pays and why now | Chill, unbothered, dependable; boring, correct, shipped |
| Prop | Stubby yellow pencil and a small sketchbook | Book bristling with sticky tabs, magnifying glass | A card with one bar; tally counter on the hip | Teacup and saucer; small leather ledger | Big coffee mug; tiny tool belt; small laptop |
| Idle | Doodles a star on its own tummy | Reads, turns a page | Squares a little stack of cards | Slow sip of tea, steam curling | Slow sip of coffee, eyes closed in bliss |
| Working | Scribbles fast, tongue out, eraser crumbs fly | Peers through the magnifying glass | Rules one bar on graph paper | Writes neat sums, a coin beside the ledger | Types steadily |
| Thinking | Taps the pencil on its chin, eyes up | Pushes glasses up, looks over them | Counts on its nubs | Stirs the tea, eyes half closed | Squints one eye at the screen, mug paused |
| Signature move | Flips the sketchbook round to show the doodle | Pulls a sticky tab off and holds it up: "found it" | Holds the one card up high, small proud nod | Raises the teacup like a gentle toast | Shuts the laptop with a pat, lazy thumbs up: "shipped" |
| When she speaks | Leans in fast, pencil ready | Peers over the glasses, nods slowly | Goes very still, listening | Sets the cup down, warm smile | Opens one eye, then the other |

Every character also breathes at its own pace and blinks at its own rhythm; whoever speaks, the others glance at.
