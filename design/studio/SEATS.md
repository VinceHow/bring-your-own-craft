# Any seat is hers: how the table is cast

Vincent, 14 Nov (evening). A proposal for the product owner and the build lead: they decide scope and build. Design owns how it looks.

## The idea
She can take **any** seat: Product, Design, Research, Engineering, Data, Strategy, or Your own craft. The team fills
every other seat. The five painted teammates are **people, not job titles**: each keeps their name, face and
personality, and wears whichever role the table needs. No new art is needed.

## The table
- **Core crafts, always at the table:** Product, Design, Research, Engineering. If she takes one of them, a teammate takes
  the one she would have needed.
- **Flex seat(s):** whatever else fits *this* idea. Data and Strategy are the defaults, but the seat can become a role that fits
  better (for a bakery: Operations, or the regulars' voice). It's generated for the scenario.
- Her seat plus five teammates make six. The table is always full; no empty or "invite a teammate" seat.

## Who wears what (simple, explainable rule)
The teammate whose craft she takes moves to **Product**. If she takes a flex seat or brings her own craft, **Ines moves to
Product**: the explorer with the map reads naturally as product discovery.

| She takes | Mika | Noor | Bo | Theo | Ines |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product (default, the demo) | Design | Research | Engineering | Data | Strategy |
| Design | **Product** | Research | Engineering | Data | Strategy |
| Research | Design | **Product** | Engineering | Data | Strategy |
| Engineering | Design | Research | **Product** | Data | Strategy |
| Data | Design | Research | Engineering | *flex* | **Product** |
| Strategy | Design | Research | Engineering | Data | **Product** |
| Your own craft (e.g. a baker) | Design | Research | Engineering | *flex* | **Product** |

*flex* = the generated role that fits the idea. Theo keeps his little card with one bar, which reads well for operations,
measurement or any "keeps track" role.

Props still read: Mika's sketchbook works for product journeys, Noor's tabbed book for customer notes, Bo's laptop for
specs, Ines's map for discovery. The mood clips are craft-neutral (working, thinking, talking, a note, delighted, celebrating).

## The trick that makes it feel real
During "The team walks in", the **fixed** teammates are introduced first (about 2 s each). While they appear, one fast call
writes the flex teammate's role and card from her craft sentence and idea. That teammate is introduced **last**
("…and for this one, Theo looks after the morning bake list"), so the wait is hidden in the intro.

**If the call isn't back yet, no fallback: be honest, with a smile** (Vincent). The empty chair gets a small inked note, e.g.
"Theo's running a little late: we're finding you someone who knows morning bake lists." The flow never waits: she carries on
(her character card, her idea), and Theo pops into the chair with the new card the moment it's ready, even mid-flow. No
spinner, no timer, nothing that nags. If the call fails outright, the note stays light ("Still on the way") and the run goes
on with four teammates plus her; nothing pretends.

## Rules that hold
- **"Brings" stays true.** A generated role may only promise what that teammate's prompt and tools really do. Change the line,
  never the claim.
- **Questions follow her seat.** Her domains come from what she said, so a designer gets questions about the feel and the
  flow, and an engineer about how it should behave, never about logins or fonts. The cap of 3 questions stands.
- **No pronouns**, names and the "agent" mark stay, and moods come only from real events.

## Design changes this implies (design studio)
- **Pick card:** all crafts plus Your own craft, as small inked pills. Product is first and prefilled on the stage path.
- **Pills and cards:** the role label is per run. The name and face stay; the role line changes.
- **Team intro:** the fixed teammates first, the flex one last, with its card writing itself in.
