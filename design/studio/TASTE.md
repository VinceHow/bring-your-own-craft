# How Vincent judges design: the rules we design by

Drawn from Vincent's calls on 14 Nov. When a gap needs designing and he isn't around, design it to these rules, then
show him pictures. Bring him only what changes the demo, needs new spend, or breaks a cut line.

## The feel
- **A cozy studio, not a meeting.** Hand-painted gouache, warm, calm, mature. Nothing childish, no points, meters or scores.
- **Her flow is sacred.** One thing at a time. Nothing pings, counts up, nags or waits on her. While she's focused,
  anything new waits until she's back in the room.
- **Show, don't tell.** Changes appear in the artifact itself; the team's comments arrive, are seen, and fade.
  Records (chat, notes) stay hidden by default, one tap away.
- **Honest, with a smile.** When something is slow or missing, say so plainly and kindly ("Theo's running a little
  late…"). No fake fallbacks, no spinners, and the flow never waits.

## The look
- **Ink-paper:** cream paper, a hand-inked wobbly outline, a crayon underlayer in the owner's colour.
  **No square corners. No hard boxes.**
- **No glows, rings or hover halos.** A chair is just a chair. Hover shows as a small lift or tilt at most.
- **Playful objects are Easter eggs:** discoverable, never signposted.
- **Night is really night:** dark, rainy, lamp-lit, lo-fi.
- **Each teammate has a personality:** their own corner, props and voice. Never repeat a background or a pose.
- **Composition matters:** centre the subject and keep the UI clear of faces. No UI or text baked into generated art.

## The team
- **Chime-ins only from teammates whose craft is touched:** 1 is normal, 3 is rare. Never forced, and never ending in
  a question to her.
- **The team is out of view while she captures her thoughts.** Nobody stares at her.
- **She is the expert:** questions draw on her craft. Her words are cleaned, never reworded.

## Delight
- **Spend the polish where the demo starts and where it lands:** the first 10 seconds (intro, door reveal, taps) and
  the close.
- **Every element should reward a curious click.**
- **Arrivals and departures are visible and calm:** paper arcs, folds, a tray that rises to take a note. No bounce,
  no sound unless music is already on.

## Money and honesty
- **fal spend has a ceiling** set by Vincent, every call goes in `design/FAL-SPEND.md`, and no asset is wasted.
  Ask before going over.
- **Never call it done until it's been seen running.** Say what wasn't tested.
