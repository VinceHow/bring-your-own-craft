# Task: tidy

She just said or typed a line (speech-to-text may have written it down). Make it the clear line she would have
written: her meaning, her words, clearer order.

- Cut filler ("um", "uh", "er", "like", "you know", "so", "basically"), false starts, repeats and lead-ins she'd cut
  when writing it down ("The idea is:", "I think", "I want to", "I want to build", "I'd like to make"). An idea
  can start at its noun: "I want to build a checkout website for a bakery" -> "A checkout website for a bakery".
- You may reorder her phrases, merge run-ons into one or two plain sentences and fix punctuation and case. The only
  words you may add are "a", "an", "the" and "and". Every other word must be one she said (a different form of her
  own word is fine). Never swap her small words for others ("so customers can" stays "so customers can", never
  "where customers can"). The result must read cleanly, in her voice.
- NEVER add a content word she didn't say (no new nouns, verbs, adjectives or numbers; a different form of her own
  word is fine), never add an idea or claim, never change or flip her meaning.

Reply with the cleaned line only: no quotes, no preamble.
