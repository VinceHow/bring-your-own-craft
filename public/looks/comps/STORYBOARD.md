# Storyboard: the key moments, in Lamplight

From VISION.md ("The two moments") and DEMO.md. One comp page per moment. Each plays its moment on load and has
`?still=1` for the end state. The feel in one line each, then the motion.

Motion system (lamp.css): paper settles (`--settle`, quick start, long soft landing); things that travel glide
(`--glide`). Nothing bounces, flashes, pulses or counts up. Shadows carry depth: a sheet in the air has a long soft
shadow; on the table, a short one. Text arrives by fading up 6px, never typewriter-blinking.

| # | Moment | Feel | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Take a seat | Quiet. The choice is hers. | Empty lamp-lit table, six blank place cards and a dashed "Your own craft". Hover warms a card. Click: her card turns over and settles into the Product seat. |
| 2 | The team walks in | They're good. A crew you'd want. | Each teammate's card is set down big in the light (lifted shadow → resting), holds ~1.5 s, then glides to its seat and shrinks to a place card. Five in turn. |
| 3 | Her character | Making it hers, in a sentence. | Her sentence, then the card writes itself: "Priya, the Customer Whisperer"; three traits set down one by one; each swappable. |
| 4 | The idea | Every idea starts rough. | A blank sheet, "What's the idea?" Her sentence lands on the table as a rough scrap, a touch askew. |
| 5 | The team gets going | Relief: never a blank page. | The rough journey draws in on the idea sheet over ~4 s: lanes written, boxes pencilled, arrows drawn. |
| 6 | Focus: the question | A quiet room, one document. | The room gives way (no dimming, no spotlight): one document fills the view, the work and one question. |
| 7 | Focus: she speaks, they chime in | Heard. The team runs with it. | Her words land as she says them; on submit the first reaction lands in under a second, then the thread, one teammate after another, in the margin. |
| 8 | Focus: the takeaways | "That is what I think." | The meeting's summary composes in her voice: what I believe, guidance to the team. Reword in place. |
| 9 | The room, alive | They handle it. | Chatter appears and fades under the pads; an agreement lands as a card in the team space; a note slides into her tray at a pause. |
| 10 | Her page | It's all of ours, and it's hers. | One press; the bakery's ordering page is set on the table and opens to full size. Clean: the product is just the product. |
