Privacy

When you book a class we keep your name, your email, the class and, if you give it, your phone number. We use them to hold your place, send the confirmation and tell you if a class changes.

"Remember me on this device" keeps your usual class in your own browser only. Clear it any time by clearing this site's data.

We never sell or share your details. To have them deleted, email the studio.
