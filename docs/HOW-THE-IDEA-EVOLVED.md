# How the idea evolved (Sat 14 Nov)

The product owner's record of how the experience changed during the day, for `BUILT-THIS-WAY.md` and the submission
write-up. The problem never changed; what changed is how it feels to be inside the product. Each turn came from
Vincent, in a back-and-forth with the product-owner session; the build lead priced and merged each one.

**Times are UTC**, taken from the product owner's commits on its `po/*` branches (each is when the agreed change was
written into `DEMO.md` / `BRIEF.md`; the conversation behind it was a few minutes earlier). Times before 12:28 come
from `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

## The six turns

1. **A tool with a rule** (morning, to ~12:10). We started from a contrast: AI builders guess everything, coding
   agents ask about everything. Our answer was a cap: at most three questions, enforced in code. The baseline proved
   the point: a default agent asked 0 questions and guessed 22 things about a yoga studio (`docs/baseline/`).
2. **A room of crafts** (12:28–13:19). The cap became a room. You take the seat of the craft you love and agents fill
   the other seats. She picks her own seat, the team assembles with what each loves and brings, and a Strategy seat is
   added so commercial judges see themselves at the table. Her own card follows, and voice moves into core.
3. **A studio and a team you'd love** (13:52–14:15). Early user feedback: "it feels like walking into a work
   meeting". The room became a cozy studio, and the point became the team dynamic: they're good, they handle their
   own work, and everyone builds your rough idea up together. The gem stays a metaphor; credits go; a calm tray and
   "nothing breaks her focus" settle what reaches her.
4. **An autonomous team** (14:24–14:41). Teammates get names (Mika, Noor, Theo, Ines, Bo), anyone can build their own
   seat ("Your own craft"), and the team assumes, fills gaps and keeps moving from a private picture of you it keeps
   updating.
5. **Questions became focus moments** (14:41–15:13). Options and branches went. A question reaches you only if it's
   big and draws on what you're good at or love; it opens as one document with one thoughtful question. You say your
   piece at your own pace, the team chimes in like a real meeting the instant you submit, and the meeting's takeaways
   sum it up in your voice. Your deeper philosophy is the team's to keep, behind the scenes.
6. **The thinking shows in the studio, not on the product** (15:38–15:50). Joint decisions are really talked out
   between teammates and land in a team space; the product is just the product. Priya, a product manager, building an
   ordering site for a friend's bakery, is locked in as the persona and the demo idea, and `VISION.md` becomes the
   one-pager every session reads. Vincent declared the vision final at about 15:51.
